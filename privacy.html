Privacy Policy — Claygram

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Claygram does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

The game runs entirely on your device. Your progress, coins, and settings are saved only in the app's local storage on your device and never leave it.

Claygram contains no ads, no analytics, and no third-party tracking.

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. Your payment information is handled by Google and is never received or stored by the developer.

If you contact us by email, your message is used only to reply to you.
